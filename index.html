🛡️Data privacy & user terms

Plain-language guidance for using this Power AI prototype.

🔐 Data privacy notice

  • Local demo storage: profile and feedback entries may be saved in your browser using local storage on this device.
  • No server delivery: this prototype does not send forms to a Power AI server and does not provide a secure account system.
  • Minimise data: provide only information needed to test features. Use sample details if possible.
  • Never submit: passwords, OTPs, recovery codes, Aadhaar/passport/ID numbers, bank/card details, private keys, or confidential records.
  • Shared devices: people with access to this browser may see locally saved entries. Clear this site's browser data to remove the demo's local storage.
  • External services: external websites and image services have their own privacy practices and policies.

📘 User terms & conditions

  • Prototype only: Power AI provides general career and learning guidance for demonstration and educational purposes.
  • No guarantee: career suggestions and job links may be incomplete or outdated. Verify job details directly with employers.
  • Responsible use: do not use the site to share or request another person's private information.
  • External services: Power AI does not control third-party websites or their content.
  • No professional advice: this prototype is not legal, financial, employment, or security advice and does not guarantee a job or outcome.
  • Changes: features and these prototype terms may change during development.

This is a starter privacy notice and terms summary, not legal advice or a substitute for a reviewed policy. Review it for your project and applicable laws before public or commercial use.
